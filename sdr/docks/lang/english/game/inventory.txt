Inventory.

Your inventory holds the items you pick up during a run: ammo packs, gold packs, keys, steel shields, life capsules, health potions and player teleporters, along with any specials you buy in the player shop. You move through it and use what you need without stopping, since there's no inventory menu. Your inventory starts empty every run.

How your inventory works.

Items in your inventory are kept in alphabetical order, with one entry for each kind, however many you have. Powerups never go in your inventory.
Each time you move to an item, you hear a sound placed from left to right by where it sits in the list, and its name is spoken, with how many you have when there's more than one, and its place in the list, such as "ammo packs, 3. Item 1 of 2." The list wraps around, so moving past the last item takes you back to the first.
If your inventory is empty, you're told so.

The keys.

Shift plus tab or I: Moves to the previous item in your inventory.
Tab or O: Moves to the next item in your inventory.
Enter or U: Uses the item you're on.

On a joystick, the left stick click moves to the previous item, the right stick click to the next, and the B button uses the item you're on.

Using an item.

Using the item you're on does what that item does: opening an ammo pack or a gold pack, unlocking a door with a key, putting on a shield, using a life capsule, a health potion or a player teleporter. The player shop's specials are explained in its document.
An item is only used up if it does something. An ammo pack with no gun to fill, a key with no locked door in reach, a life capsule at your most lives or a health potion at full health stays in your inventory for later. With nothing in your inventory, you're told you don't have anything to use.
While a door carries you, a ladder or a floor breaker holds you, or a slide carries you, you can't use your inventory.

Your inventory in practice and free runs.

A practice run starts you with a steel shield, except when practicing hazards or floor breakers, and keys turn up for real doors.

In a free run, modify player inventory gives, sets or takes any item or special, with give items adding to what you have, set items setting exactly how many you carry, and take items removing some.
