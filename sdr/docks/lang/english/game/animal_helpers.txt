Animal helpers.

An animal helper claps for you, so you never have to press both hands together to scare an animal away. It's a special you buy in the player shop for 3,000 gold coins.

How an animal helper works.

While it's on, it claps as soon as an animal comes within reach of your hands, and the animal runs off, just as if you'd clapped yourself. You'll hear the clap and the animal being scared, and it only claps when there's an animal to scare.
It waits during a slide or a trampoline flight, since either one carries you past animals safely.

Using one.

Choose it in your inventory and use it to switch it on or off, and you'll hear its sound and be told which. Your inventory also says whether it's on, such as "animal helper, on." You can still clap by hand while it's off.

Animal helpers in a run.

An animal helper is never used up. It lasts for the rest of the run, even if you lose a life, and extra copies don't make it any stronger. It's gone when the run ends, along with the rest of your inventory.
Every animal it scares counts in your statistics, just as if you'd clapped yourself.

Animal helpers in practice and free runs.

Animal helpers aren't available in practice or timed runs.

In a free run, you can give, set or take animal helpers from modify player inventory, so you can try one without spending anything.
