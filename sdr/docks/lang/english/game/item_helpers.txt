Item helpers.

An item helper picks up items for you, so you don't have to press a hand for each one or risk breaking it by running onto it. It's a special you buy in the player shop for 4,000 gold coins.

How an item helper works.

While it's on, every item in your lane is picked up as soon as a hand could reach it, keys and gold packs included, and goes into your inventory. You still need to be in the item's lane, as items in other lanes just pass you by.

Using one.

Choose it in your inventory and use it to switch it on or off, and you'll hear its sound and be told which. Your inventory also says whether it's on, such as "item helper, on." You can still pick up items by hand while it's off.

Item helpers in a run.

An item helper is never used up. It lasts for the rest of the run, even if you lose a life, and extra copies don't make it any stronger. It's gone when the run ends, along with the rest of your inventory.
Every item it picks up counts in your statistics, just as if you'd picked it up yourself.

Item helpers in practice and free runs.

Item helpers aren't available in practice or timed runs.

In a free run, you can give, set or take item helpers from modify player inventory, so you can try one without spending anything.
