Player shop.

The player shop is where you spend your gold coins during an endless run, on items and specials that go straight into your inventory.

Getting there.

Press slash or F8 during an endless run to enter the player shop. The run pauses while you're inside, and picks up where it left off when you leave. Press enter to skip the sound as you enter or leave. On a joystick, you can give it a button in the joystick tab of the settings menu. The player shop isn't open in timed, practice or free runs.
Your gold coins come from gold packs, as described under gold packs, and are saved from one run to the next. Press Y during a run to hear how many you have available to spend.

Buying something.

The player shop has two categories, items and specials. Each one lists what it sells, with its price and how many you can afford, such as "health potion, costs 1,000 gold coins. You can buy 3."
Choosing one opens its own menu, which tells you how many gold coins you have. It offers packs of 1, 5, 10, 25, 50 and 100, each with its full price, then buy all, which buys as many as you can afford.
What you buy goes straight into your inventory, and you stay on the same line so you can buy more. If you used buy all, or can't afford another, a message tells you what you bought and takes you back to the list.
If you can't afford something, you'll hear an error and be told so, and nothing is bought. A category won't open if you can't afford anything in it.

Items.

Items work just as they do when you find them on the belt, as each one's document explains. Keys and gold packs aren't sold.

Ammo pack: 500 gold coins.
Health potion: 1,000 gold coins.
Life capsule: 5,000 gold coins.
Player teleporter: 1,500 gold coins.
Steel shield: 2,000 gold coins.

Specials.

Specials are only ever bought, and never turn up on the belt. They wait in your inventory until you use them, and like everything in your inventory, they're gone when the run ends.

Door helper: 3,500 gold coins. While it's on, it unlocks each real door with your first key once the door is in reach, then opens it as soon as a hand could. With no key, it tries the door once, and you hear that it's locked. It leaves fake doors for you to break.
Item helper: 4,000 gold coins. While it's on, every item in your lane is picked up as soon as a hand could reach it, keys and gold packs included. Items in other lanes still pass you by.
Slide: 1,250 gold coins. Using one puts you on a slide straight away.
Trampoline: 1,750 gold coins. Using one launches you on a good trampoline's flight from where you are.

Using specials.

Using a helper switches it on or off, and your inventory tells you which, such as "item helper, on." A helper is never used up. It lasts for the rest of the run, even if you lose a life, and extra copies don't make it any stronger.
A slide or a trampoline is used up when you use it. You can't use one while a slide or a trampoline flight is already carrying you, and it stays in your inventory for later.

The player shop in your statistics.

What you spend counts toward gold coins spent in your statistics.

Specials in free runs.

There's no player shop in a free run, but modify player inventory can give, set or take any of the specials, so you can try them without spending anything.
