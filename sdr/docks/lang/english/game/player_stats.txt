Player stats.

Your player stats are attack, defence, max health, max shield strength and shield defence. You buy them in the player shop's character tab to hit harder, last longer and take less damage for the rest of your run. Every run starts with 0 points in each.

What each one does.

Attack: each point adds 1 damage to every hit from any weapon, melee or gun. With 10 attack, the bat goes from 12 damage a hit to 22, and the pistol from 120 to 130.
Defence: each point takes 1 off every blow to your health. With 10 defence, a door on chaos does 60 instead of 70.
Max health: each point adds 1 to your full health and 1 to your current health. If you're at 150 of 200 and buy 50, you'll be at 200 of 250, and each new life refills to your raised maximum.
Max shield strength: each point adds 1 to your shield's full strength, and 1 to its current strength if you have one in use. Every new shield you start gets the bonus too, so with 100 points it starts at 500 instead of 400.
Shield defence: each point takes 1 off every blow to a shield you're wearing. With 10 shield defence, a door on chaos takes 60 off your shield instead of 70. Your regular defence doesn't protect your shield, and shield defence doesn't protect your health.

How they work with damage.

A blow is worked out in order: first your level's share of its damage, as described under difficulty, then any powerup that halves or doubles it, then your defence or shield defence comes off. A blow always does at least 1 damage, however many points you have.
Since your points take off the same amount on every level, they make a bigger difference on easier levels. With 10 defence, a spike does 2 on easy and 10 on chaos.
Your points can't save you from falling into a hazard or a floor breaker, which still end your run straight away. Only a safety net can save you from those, as its document explains.

Buying them.

Press slash or F8 during an endless or free run to enter the player shop, then choose character, which comes first. Each point costs the following:

Attack: 100 gold coins.
Defence: 150 gold coins.
Max health: 50 gold coins.
Max shield strength: 25 gold coins.
Shield defence: 200 gold coins.

Each one has the usual packs of 1, 5, 10, 25, 50, 100, 500 and 1,000, then buy all, and a pack of 5 gives you 5 points. There's no limit to how many points you can have.
Your points work the moment you buy them, and they don't go into your inventory, so there's nothing to choose or use.
Max shield strength and shield defence are only listed while you have a steel shield in your inventory, on or off. Any points you've already bought stay yours when a shield breaks, and work again on your next one.

Checking your points.

Press P for the pause menu and choose view session statuses, which lists your attack, defence, max health, max shield strength and shield defence.

Player stats in a run.

Your points last through lost lives, and they're gone when the run ends. Replaying or restarting a run starts you back at 0.
What you spend on them in an endless run counts toward gold coins spent in your statistics.

Player stats in practice and free runs.

Player stats aren't available in practice or timed runs.

In a free run, you can buy them in the player shop with that run's own gold coins, or modify player stats in the console can give, set or take your attack, defence and max health, and your max shield strength and shield defence while you have a shield, so you can try them without spending anything. Set takes a number from 0 to 1000, and take stops at 0. Clear belt leaves your points alone.
Max health and max shield strength are bonuses on top of the full amounts you set with modify player health and modify shield strength. Those two settings stay through a replay, while your points go back to 0.
Taking max health or max shield strength points lowers your maximum, but your current amount only drops if it no longer fits, so taking points can never cost you a life or break your shield. With 100 max health points, 250 of 300 becomes 200 of 200, while 150 of 300 becomes 150 of 200.
