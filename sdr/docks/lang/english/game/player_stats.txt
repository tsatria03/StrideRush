Player stats.

Your player stats are attack, defence and shield defence. You buy them in the player shop's character category to hit harder and take less damage for the rest of your run. Every run starts with 0 points in each.

What each one does.

Attack: each point adds 1 damage to every hit from any weapon, melee or gun. With 10 attack, the bat goes from 12 damage a hit to 22, and the pistol from 120 to 130.
Defence: each point takes 1 off every blow to your health. With 10 defence, a door on chaos does 60 instead of 70.
Shield defence: each point takes 1 off every blow to a shield you're wearing. With 10 shield defence, a door on chaos takes 60 off your shield instead of 70. Your regular defence doesn't protect your shield, and shield defence doesn't protect your health.

How they work with damage.

A blow is worked out in order: first your level's share of its damage, as described under difficulty, then any powerup that halves or doubles it, then your defence or shield defence comes off. A blow always does at least 1 damage, however many points you have.
Since your points take off the same amount on every level, they make a bigger difference on easier levels. With 10 defence, a spike does 2 on easy and 10 on chaos.
Your points can't save you from falling into a hazard or a floor breaker, which still end your run straight away.

Buying them.

Press slash or F8 during an endless run to enter the player shop, then choose character, which comes first. Each point costs:

Attack: 100 gold coins.
Defence: 200 gold coins.
Shield defence: 300 gold coins.

Each one has the usual packs of 1, 5, 10, 25, 50 and 100, then buy all, and a pack of 5 gives you 5 points. There's no limit to how many points you can have.
Your points work the moment you buy them, and they don't go into your inventory, so there's nothing to choose or use.
Shield defence is only listed while you have a steel shield in your inventory or are wearing one. Any shield defence you've already bought stays yours when a shield breaks, and works again on your next one.

Checking your points.

Press P for the pause menu and choose view session statuses, which lists your attack, defence and shield defence.

Player stats in a run.

Your points last through lost lives, and they're gone when the run ends. Replaying or restarting a run starts you back at 0.
What you spend on them counts toward gold coins spent in your statistics.

Player stats in practice and free runs.

Player stats aren't available in practice or timed runs.

In a free run, modify player stats in the console can give, set or take your attack and defence, and your shield defence while you have a shield, so you can try them without spending anything. Set takes a number from 0 to 1000, and take stops at 0. Clear belt leaves your points alone.
