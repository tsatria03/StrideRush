Endless runs.

An endless run is the main way to play. You choose a difficulty, and the belt carries you on, getting faster as you go, with more kinds of obstacle joining, until something stops you. The goal is to get as far as you can.

Starting one.

Endless run is in play game, and opens once you've jumped over your first 5 hazards while practicing them, since a hazard is the one obstacle that ends a run outright. Choosing it asks for one of the five difficulty levels, easy, medium, hard, insane or chaos, as described under difficulty.

How an endless run works.

You start with 1 life and 200 health, in a random environment, at your level's starting speed. Every 10 seconds you get a little faster, until you reach your level's top speed. As you speed up, new kinds of obstacle and item join, and a real door marks the start of each faster stretch.
The run ends when you lose your last life, or straight away if you fall into a hazard or a floor breaker.

Gold coins and the player shop.

Gold packs turn up along the way in an endless run, and opening them gives you gold coins, which are saved from one run to the next. Press Y to hear how many you have, and slash or F8 to spend them in the player shop, which pauses the run while you're inside. The gold packs and player shop documents explain both.

Pausing and quitting.

Press P for the pause menu, where you can resume, restart or quit the run, view your session statuses, or view your session results, the stats for the run so far, and copy them to your clipboard or export them to a file. Pressing escape asks straight away whether you want to quit, and the run stops moving while you decide. Restarting or quitting asks you first, since you'll lose the run you're on.

When a run ends.

When an endless run ends in a death, your results for the run come up straight away, starting with the difficulty and your death outcome, which tells you what killed you. Pressing escape or back then asks what you'd like to do now: copy or export your results, replay the run on the same difficulty, or return to the game menu. Copying or exporting brings you back to the same question, so you can do both before you choose.
The game tab of the settings menu can turn off the results, so the question comes straight after you die, starting with "You have died", and can leave copy and export out of the question.

What counts.

Your statistics only count endless runs, and are saved whenever an endless run ends, however it ends. Practice runs and free runs never change your statistics or earn achievements.
A run counts toward unlocking the next difficulty only if you opened a real door and got through it before you died. Finishing 5 such runs on a level opens the next one, 5 easy runs also open timed runs, and 5 hard runs open free runs.
