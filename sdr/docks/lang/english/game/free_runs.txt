Free runs.

A free run is a sandbox: you build the run yourself, adding exactly what you want to the belt and changing your speed, environment, health and more at any time. It has no difficulty, and nothing you do in it counts toward your stats, achievements, records or unlocks.

Starting one.

Free run, in play game after timed run, opens once you've finished 5 hard endless runs, the same time insane opens. First you choose a running speed: 2, 2.5, 3.3, 5 or 10 steps a second, normal, which speeds up as an endless run does, or rising, which climbs from 2 to 10 steps a second. Then you choose an environment to stay in, or random.
The run starts with you standing still on an empty belt, hearing only the music and ambience, with no footsteps or heartbeat. Every key still works, so you can try jumping, crouching or your hands in place. Your guns start with 50 rounds in reserve, as in an endless run, and your inventory starts empty.

The console.

Press backslash or F7 to open the free run console, which freezes the run while it's open and lists its choices in alphabetical order. Standing still, it has modify death mode, modify environment, modify footsteps, modify heartbeat, modify practice hints, modify running speed, modify spacing, start running and view current setup.
Once you choose start running, your footsteps and heartbeat begin and the rest of the console appears: add items, add obstacles, add specials, clear belt, clear items, clear obstacles, clear specials, modify ammo limits and modify ammo reserves while a gun is drawn, modify player health, modify player inventory, modify player lives, modify player specials, modify player stats, and modify shield strength while your shield is on or after you've taken it off.
Every change closes the console and tells you what it did in the misc buffer, while view current setup lists your speed, spacing, death mode, footsteps, heartbeat, hints, environment, a slide you're riding and any powerups running, with how long they have left, and everything coming, one line for each kind.

Adding to the belt.

Add obstacles, add items and add specials each ask which kind, or all of them for a random mix, then how many, from 1 to 1000. Obstacles are every kind you can practice, plus floor breakers and vehicles, items are the ones that go in your inventory, and specials are powerups and slides. For fake walls, fake doors, vehicles, aircrafts and insects, you can also give each one its own health, from 1 to 2000, or leave it empty for the normal health for your speed.
Barriers, vehicles, aircrafts, trampolines and every item, or all obstacles or all items, also ask for a lane: the left, middle or right lane, your lane, which puts each one in whichever lane you're in as it appears, or random lanes as usual. Choosing combinations first asks which combination, from each pair your environment can make in either order, or random combinations.
Last, you choose whether they spawn continuously. If not, that many come down the belt one after another, after anything already waiting, and then stop. If so, that kind keeps coming for as long as you run, and the number decides how often it comes compared with your other continuous kinds, so 10 barriers and 5 vehicles bring barriers about twice as often. A real door always brings its key first.
Each add list only has the kinds your current environment has, and if it has none, the console tells you so, such as "This environment has no obstacles." Anything you've added that the environment you're in can't bring waits until you're somewhere that has it, without being used up, and what can come goes ahead of it.

Clearing the belt.

Clear obstacles, clear items and clear specials list only the kinds still coming, such as "barriers, 7 left", "vehicles, continuous" or "fake walls, 3 left, plus continuous", then all of them together, and remove that kind's batches, its continuous spawning and any already on the belt.
Clear belt clears everything coming and on the belt, empties your inventory, takes off your shield, resets your weapons, ends any powerups and stands you still again, while keeping your settings.

Your settings.

Each modify choice tells you what it's currently set to, or how much you currently have, as you open it, and its menu starts on your current choice.
Modify death mode chooses no deaths, where your last life is never lost and your health just refills, while a hazard or a floor breaker sets you down just past it and sends another one down the belt; normal deaths, where losing your last life or falling into a hazard or a floor breaker ends the run; or practice deaths, which set you down just past what caught you with your lives back.
Modify spacing chooses how far apart things come on the belt: very close, with only a couple of steps between one thing and the next one's warning, close, normal, which is how a free run starts, far or very far, with up to 10 seconds between them. A change applies from the next gap on.
Modify environment chooses an environment to stay in, or random, modify running speed chooses your speed, modify footsteps chooses heard or silent footsteps, modify heartbeat chooses a heard or silent heartbeat, and modify practice hints chooses silent or spoken hints for whatever you've added.

Your health, lives, items and specials.

Modify player health, modify player lives and modify shield strength each offer give, set and take. Set takes a number, from 1 to 2000 for your health and your shield, or 1 to 1000 for your lives, which becomes your full health, your lives, or your shield's full strength. Give adds to what you have, up to full, and take removes from it, stopping at 1, so it never costs a life or breaks your shield. To hear the amounts you've set when you press H and M, choose amount, or percentage and amount, for the health and shield formats in the game tab of the settings menu.
Modify player inventory gives you any number of any item or player shop special, choosing keys by color, sets exactly how many of one you carry, from 0 to 1000, or takes items away, listing what you're carrying with how many of each.
Modify ammo limits chooses limited or unlimited rounds for the drawn gun, and while it's limited, modify ammo reserves gives, sets or takes its rounds, from 0 to 1000.
Modify player specials has give powerup and give slide. Give powerup lists every powerup by name, the two shield ones only while you wear a shield, and starts the one you choose straight away, just as if you'd collected it, for its usual 15 seconds, replacing one of the same kind. Give slide puts you on a slide right where you are, for its usual 2 to 4 seconds, or starts a fresh ride if you're already on one, and tells you if your environment has no slides.
Modify player stats gives, sets or takes your attack and defence, and your shield defence while you have a shield, from 0 to 1000, as the player stats document explains.

Replaying a free run.

If a free run ends in a death, replay run starts the same run again, standing still, with your speed, environment, settings, continuous spawning and every batch back at its full count, and restarting from the pause menu does the same.
