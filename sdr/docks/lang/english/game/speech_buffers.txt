Speech buffers.

Everything the game speaks during a run is kept in speech buffers, so you can go back and review it. You can move through the messages, copy them, mute a kind of message you don't want to hear, or save a buffer to a file.

The buffers.

The all buffer holds every message, and the rest each hold one kind:

Achievements, for the achievements you earn.
Doors, for locked, jammed and unlocked doors.
Items, for what you pick up, your inventory and what your items do.
Misc, for your music and ambience volume, the environments you enter, anything you unlock in a practice run, and what you change in a free run's console.
Powerups, for the powerups you collect and when they wear off.
Practice, for the hints in a practice run.
Status, for your health, shield, lane, distance, speed and running time.
Weapons, for drawing, reloading and your ammo.

A buffer only shows up once something has been spoken into it, and they come in alphabetical order after all.

The keys.

Comma: Moves to the previous message in the current buffer.
Period: Moves to the next message in the current buffer.
Shift plus comma: Moves to the first message in the current buffer.
Shift plus period: Moves to the last message in the current buffer.
Left bracket: Moves to the previous buffer.
Right bracket: Moves to the next buffer.
Shift plus left bracket: Moves to the first buffer.
Shift plus right bracket: Moves to the last buffer.
Shift plus M: Mutes or unmutes the current buffer.
Shift plus C: Copies the current message to the clipboard.
Alt plus shift plus C: Opens the current message in a box you can read with the arrow keys.
Shift plus B: Brings up the buffer menu.
Shift plus backslash: Exports the current buffer to a text file, then clears it.

Muting a buffer.

A muted buffer still keeps its messages, so you can review them, but they aren't spoken as they happen. A muted buffer stays muted the next time you play, until you unmute it. The all buffer can't be muted.

The buffer menu.

The buffer menu lets you copy, export, mute or review a buffer or a single message, all in one place.
