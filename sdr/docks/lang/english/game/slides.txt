Slides.

Slides cover the whole belt, so you always go onto one. For a few seconds a slide carries you along at twice your speed, and everything you meet passes harmlessly through you, while you steer into lanes to catch items.

How a slide works.

You'll hear a slide coming ahead of you, then a sound as you get on, and for 2 to 4 seconds it carries you along at twice your speed. Your footsteps and heartbeat go quiet until you hear it end.
While you're on it you can't jump, crouch, use your hands, your inventory or your weapons, but you can switch lanes.
Obstacles still come, without their countdowns, and you pass straight through them without being hit. Ladders, floor breakers, vehicles and aircrafts pass you by too.

Riding a slide.

Any item that reaches you in the lane you're in is caught for you, so listen for the items around you and steer into their lanes.
Nothing waits right after a slide, so the next obstacle always gives you its full warning when you come off.

Slides in a run.

Slides start once the belt reaches about 3.3 steps a second, and they come less often on each harder difficulty. Only one comes at a time, and real doors and their keys wait until a slide is over. Slides never come as part of a combination.

Slides in practice and free runs.

Slides never come in practice runs.

In a free run, add slides from add specials in the console, in batches or continuously, or give yourself one straight away from modify player specials, with give slide, which puts you on a slide right where you are. View current setup tells you how long your slide has left.
