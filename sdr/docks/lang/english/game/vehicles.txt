Vehicles.

Vehicles, such as cars, drive toward you down a single lane, a little faster than the belt carries you, and chase you across the lanes. A vehicle fires bursts of shots down its lane, and close up it rams you. The way past a vehicle is to shoot it down while it's in your lane, or to switch lanes at the right moment and let it drive past.

How a vehicle works.

A vehicle starts in a random lane and moves one lane toward yours about every second until it reaches you. You hear its engine in its lane from the moment it appears, and its countdown, the same five cues as most obstacles with two notes at once, only plays while it's in your lane.
Half a second after a vehicle lines up with you, it fires a quick burst of 5 shots down its lane, then waits 2 seconds before the next. Each shot that reaches you while you're still in that lane takes 5 of your health.
About 2 steps before it reaches you, a vehicle stops shooting and comes on to ram you. If it reaches you in your lane, it takes 40 of your health and drives on past you.

Getting past a vehicle.

Your weapons can only hit a vehicle while it's in your lane. It's about half as tough as a fake wall, and once it's destroyed, its engine and any shots it still has in the air are gone.
To dodge its shots, step out of its lane as it lines up with you, and the burst flies past. To dodge the vehicle itself, switch lanes after its last move, and it drives past you harmlessly.
On a slide or a trampoline flight, a vehicle and its shots pass you by.

Vehicles in a run.

Vehicles join an endless run once the belt reaches 5 steps a second, in any environment that has them. They never come as part of a combination.
Destroying one counts toward vehicles destroyed in your statistics, letting one drive past toward vehicles dodged, a ram toward times hit by vehicles, and each shot that reaches you toward vehicle shots taken.

Vehicles in practice and free runs.

Vehicles has its own practice choice, with your weapons drawn. It opens once you've dodged 5 winds while practicing them, and getting past 5 vehicles while practicing, by destroying or dodging them, opens aircrafts. If a vehicle catches you in practice, the death message tells you how: shoot it while it's in your lane, or switch lanes after its last move so it drives past, or, for its shots, step out of its lane when it fires.

In a free run, add vehicles from add obstacles in the console, give each one its own health, or leave it empty for the normal health for your speed, and choose the lane it starts in: random lanes, as usual, your own lane, or the left, middle or right lane. Wherever it starts, it still moves toward your lane.
