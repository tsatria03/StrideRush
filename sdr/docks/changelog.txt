New in 1.4.
Made it so doors now have a warning countdown like every trap, which stops once you open a real door or break a fake one.
Added animals, birds and dogs, which call out ahead of you and run into you for 10 of your health unless you clap by pressing both hand keys together during their countdown to scare them away, and your hands now make a sound when there's nothing for them to grab.
Added health potions, which turn up more often than life capsules, and which you pick up with either hand and use with U to heal 5 to 10 percent of your health.
Made it so life potions are now called life capsules, with new sounds for finding, picking up, breaking and using them.
New in 1.3.
Made it so your settings and volumes are now remembered between launches, leaving a run now fades its sounds out instead of cutting them off, and space now also closes a dlg message.
Added a settings menu in the main menu, with general, game, keyboard, menu, sound and speech tabs, where you can rebind your keys, pick your sound device, set up and preview your voice, and choose whether escape asks before quitting a run, and F2 opens its game, menu and speech tabs during a run.
Added a key bindings screen, which you open with F1 during a run or from the settings menu, and which lists every action and the keys it's on.
Made it so melee weapons can now swing at most every fifth of a second, and the pistol fire every tenth of a second, so you can hear the weapon powerups change them.
Added powerups, which you collect by running over them on the ground or jumping into them in the air, and which give you a good or bad effect for 15 seconds, such as moving faster or slower, jumping longer or shorter, or taking less or more damage.
Made it so P is now the only key for the pause menu, and pressing escape during a run asks whether you want to quit it, with the run stopped while you decide.
Added a prompt after you die that asks whether you want to replay the current run.
Made it so the pause menu now lets you resume, restart or quit the run, and asks you first before you restart or quit.
Added a game menu, where you start a run, and a restart game option in the main menu that closes and reopens the game.
Added a documentation menu in the main menu, where you can read the changelog, credits, readme and todo list.
New in 1.2.
Made it so the H key now tells you your health and lives, and you no longer hear your own landing when a force field throws you or a teleporter moves you.
Added player health, so you now have 200 health that obstacles wear down instead of taking your lives straight away, and you only lose a life when your health runs out.
Made it so you can no longer use your inventory while a door is carrying you.
Added player teleporters, which you pick up with either hand and use with U to be thrown a random 2 to 5 seconds forward or back along the belt, sometimes right in front of danger.
Added life potions, which you pick up with either hand and drink with U for 1 extra life.
Made it so the lives key is now H instead of L, and the new M key tells you how strong your shield is.
Added steel shields, which you pick up with either hand and wear with U, and which take the damage from spikes, mines, force fields, projectiles, walls and doors in place of your lives until they break.
Made it so a fake door now sounds jammed when you try to open it with your hands, or use a key on it, so you can tell it won't open.
Made it so real doors are now locked, so you press U with a key from your inventory to unlock one from up to 2 seconds away, then open it with either hand.
Added keys, which turn up once before each real door in a choice of colors, and which you pick up with either hand, but which break if you run onto them, land on them or hit them with a weapon.
New in 1.1.
Made it so your weapons now break ammo packs, so a shot or swing at a pack in your way destroys it, and an ammo pack now gives 5 to 10 rounds to the gun you're holding, instead of 50 to both.
Made it so guns now fire bullets that fly ahead of you with their own sound, so a shot only lands if its target is within reach, and each gun now starts with 50 rounds in reserve, up to 200.
Added fake doors, which sound slightly higher or lower than a real door, can't be opened and have to be broken with your weapons, and cost 3 lives if you crash into them.
Added real doors, which mark each new speed section and carry you through when you open them with either hand, but cost 3 lives if you crash into them.
Added walls, which you have to break with your weapons before you reach them, which get tougher the faster you run, and which cost 1 life if you crash into one.
Made it so a force field's countdown now stops as soon as you switch it off.
Added over a hundred new ground surfaces to run on, such as dirt, grass, gravel, leaves, sand and stone.
Made it so you can now crouch again half a second after standing up, instead of a full second.
Made it so the ground now changes to a new surface every 10 to 20 seconds at any speed, instead of after a set distance, which made it change very rarely at the start of a run.
Made it so every trap now plays its full five-step warning countdown at any speed, instead of the countdown getting shorter as you speed up.
New in 1.0.
Added ammo packs, which you pick up with either hand as you reach them and later open from your new inventory to refill both guns' reserves, but which break if you run or land on top of them.
Added ten weapons on the number keys, from the bat up to the machine gun and pistol, which you swing or fire with the space bar, with separate ammo for each gun and reloading at any time.
Added projectiles, including missiles, cars, boulders and firewalls, which fly toward you twice as fast as you run and cost 1 life unless you crouch under them.
Added left and right hand keys, either of which you can use to switch off force fields.
Added force fields, which hit you even if you jump or crouch, cost 1 life, and throw you 5 steps forward unless you switch them off with either hand during their countdown.
Added a warning countdown before spikes and mines, the same way hazards have one, with different sounds for each kind of obstacle so you can tell which one is coming.
Added mines, which light up and start ticking as you get close, cost 2 lives if you step on one, and fade out behind you once you jump over them.
Added spikes, which swing back and forth and cost 1 life if they hit you.
Added ledges in front of every hazard, with a step-by-step countdown that tells you when the hazard is coming.
Added hazards, including pits, chasms, fire, lava, water, quicksand and electricity, which end your run if you fall into one.
Added death sounds for each kind of obstacle, after which you return to the main menu.
Added a lives system. You start each run with 1 life, and the run ends when your last life is gone.
Added crouching, which you hold to stay down, with your footsteps going quiet while you're crouched.
Added jumping, with a landing sound for the ground you come down on.
Added footstep sounds on every step, with the ground changing between carpet, cement, concrete and marble as you run.
Added speech buffers, so you can review, copy and export everything the game has spoken during a run.
Added a pause menu, where you can resume the game or quit back to the main menu.
Added keys to change the music and ambience volume during a run.
Added keys to hear your lives, distance, speed and running time.
Added endless runs that start at 500 milliseconds per step and speed up by 10 milliseconds every 10 seconds, up to a top speed of 50 milliseconds per step.
