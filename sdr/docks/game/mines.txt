Mines.

Mines lie on the belt and go off under you if you step onto one. The way past a mine is to jump over it.

How a mine works.

A mine fills the whole belt, so switching lanes won't help. It makes a sound when it appears, then lights up and starts ticking once you get close. As it comes, it plays the same five-cue countdown as most obstacles.
Stepping onto a mine takes 60 of your health.

Getting past a mine.

Jump as its last countdown cue plays to clear it. Once you're over, you'll hear it keep ticking as it fades out behind you.

Mines in a run.

Mines are in an endless run from the very start, at every speed. They can also come as half of a combination, with a barrier or with a fake door, in either order.
Jumping over one counts toward mines jumped in your statistics, and stepping onto one counts toward times hit by mines.

Mines in practice and free runs.

Mines has its own practice choice. It opens once you've jumped over 5 spikes while practicing them, and jumping over 5 mines while practicing opens projectiles.

In a free run, add mines from add obstacles in the console.
