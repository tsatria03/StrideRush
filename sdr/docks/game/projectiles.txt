Projectiles.

Projectiles, such as missiles, fly toward you at head height, twice as fast as you run, so you'll hear them rushing in. Jumping won't help: the way past a projectile is to crouch under it.

How a projectile works.

A projectile fills the whole belt, so switching lanes won't help. As it comes, it plays the same five-cue countdown as most obstacles.
If one hits you, it takes 40 of your health.

Getting past a projectile.

Hold the crouch key so you're crouched when it reaches you, and it flies over you and fades away behind. A crouch only lasts a second and a half, so at slower speeds wait for the last few countdown cues before you crouch, and stay down until it passes.

Projectiles in a run.

Projectiles join an endless run once the belt reaches about 2.2 steps a second, in any environment that has them. They can also come as half of a combination, with a spike, a barrier or an insect, in either order.
Ducking under one counts toward projectiles ducked in your statistics, and one that hits you counts toward times hit by projectiles.

Projectiles in practice and free runs.

Projectiles has its own practice choice. It opens once you've jumped over 5 mines while practicing them, and ducking under 5 projectiles while practicing opens force fields.

In a free run, add projectiles from add obstacles in the console.
