Spikes.

Spikes swing back and forth across the belt with their own rhythm, which you'll hear as you approach them. The way past a spike is to jump over it.

How a spike works.

A spike fills the whole belt, so switching lanes won't help. As it comes, it plays the same five-cue countdown as most obstacles, and you hear it swinging as it gets closer.
Getting hit by a spike takes 20 of your health.

Getting past a spike.

Jump as its last countdown cue plays to sail over it.

Spikes in a run.

Spikes are in an endless run from the very start, at every speed. They can also come as half of a combination with a projectile, in either order.
Jumping over one counts toward spikes jumped in your statistics, and one that hits you counts toward times hit by spikes.

Spikes in practice and free runs.

Spikes has its own practice choice. It opens once you've jumped over 5 hazards while practicing them, and jumping over 5 spikes while practicing opens mines.

In a free run, add spikes from add obstacles in the console.
