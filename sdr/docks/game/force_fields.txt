Force fields.

Force fields hum ahead of you while they're on, and jumping or crouching won't get you past one. The way past a force field is to switch it off with one of your hands before you reach it.

How a force field works.

A force field fills the whole belt, so it sounds straight ahead of you whichever lane you're in. As it comes, it plays the same five-cue countdown as most obstacles.
If you reach a force field while it's still on, it takes 30 of your health and throws you 5 steps forward past it.

Getting past a force field.

Press either hand during a force field's countdown to switch it off. You'll hear the switch, then the field's sound changes as it shuts off, its countdown stops, and you can run straight through.
At the fastest speeds, where the countdown is very short, you can still switch one off in the last half second before you reach it.

Force fields in a run.

Force fields join an endless run once the belt reaches 2.5 steps a second, in any environment that has them. They can also come as half of a combination, with a fake wall or with an animal, in either order. When a force field comes first, the gap after it leaves room for its throw, so a hit from it lands you before the second part, not over it.
Switching one off counts toward force fields switched off in your statistics, and one that reaches you while it's on counts toward times hit by force fields.

Force fields in practice and free runs.

Force fields has its own practice choice. It opens once you've ducked under 5 projectiles while practicing them, and switching off 5 force fields while practicing opens animals.

In a free run, add force fields from add obstacles in the console.
