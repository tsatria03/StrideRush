New in 1.0.
Added left and right hand keys, which you use to switch off force fields, with the switch sound coming from the side of the hand you used.
Added force fields, which hit you even if you jump or crouch, cost 1 life, and throw you 5 steps forward unless you switch them off with either hand during their countdown.
Added a warning countdown before spikes and mines, the same way hazards have one, with different sounds for each kind of obstacle so you can tell which one is coming.
Added mines, which light up and start ticking as you get close, cost 2 lives if you step on one, and fade out behind you once you jump over them.
Added spikes, which swing back and forth and cost 1 life if they hit you.
Added ledges in front of every hazard, with a step-by-step countdown that tells you when the hazard is coming.
Added hazards, including pits, chasms, fire, lava, water, quicksand and electricity, which end your run if you fall into one.
Added death sounds for each kind of obstacle, after which you return to the main menu.
Added a lives system. You start each run with 1 life, and the run ends when your last life is gone.
Added crouching, which you hold to stay down, with your footsteps going quiet while you're crouched.
Added jumping, with a landing sound for the ground you come down on.
Added footstep sounds on every step, with the ground changing between carpet, cement, concrete and marble as you run.
Added speech buffers, so you can review, copy and export everything the game has spoken during a run.
Added a pause menu, where you can resume the game or quit back to the main menu.
Added keys to change the music and ambience volume during a run.
Added keys to hear your lives, distance, speed and running time.
Added endless runs that start at 500 milliseconds per step and speed up by 10 milliseconds every 10 seconds, up to a top speed of 50 milliseconds per step.
