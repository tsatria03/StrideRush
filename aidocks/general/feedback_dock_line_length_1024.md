Keep every line in sr/docks/ (readme.txt, changelog.txt, credits.txt, todo list.txt) at or under 1024 characters.

**Why:** the dev and players read the docks with a screen reader that splits longer lines mid-thought.

**How to apply:** break long paragraphs into shorter lines when writing or editing a dock file. Related: [[feedback_changelog_rules]].
