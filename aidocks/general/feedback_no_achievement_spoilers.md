Player-facing docs never name an achievement or say what earns it (dev, 2026-11-19, on the first game documents: "Please do not spoial achievements when you write the game docks. Players should figure out how to earn them.", quoting the animals document's "Scaring away 10 animals over all your runs earns Off You Go, and 100 earns Animal Whisperer.").

**Why:** finding out how to earn achievements is part of the game; the achievements menu already gives hints for locked ones.

**How to apply:** in sdr/docks/game/ documents and the readme, a statistic may be mentioned (what counts toward it), but no achievement names, thresholds or "earns" lines. The achievements document may explain how achievements work in general (session and lifetime, the menu, hints) without listing any. Related: [[project_readme_split_plan]].
