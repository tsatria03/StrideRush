A yes/no menu has exactly two items, "Yes" then "No". All context goes in the prompt line, never in the labels.

**Why:** short, predictable labels are fast to hear, and players learn that Yes is first.

**How to apply:** "Are you sure you want to quit?" with Yes and No. Escape still speaks "canceled" ([[feedback_menus_say_canceled]]).
