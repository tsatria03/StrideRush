When a plan has player-facing changes, propose its changelog entry (or entries) during planning and get the wording agreed before the dev commits the plan (dev, 2026-11-18, on the free run spacing plan: "Review the change log wording first, then I'll commit. Do that for a plan before I commit it.").

**Why:** the dev wants the player-facing description settled with the design, not left for the docks section at the end.

**How to apply:** after the sections are proposed (and before saying the plan is settled), propose the entry wording as its own question; log the agreed wording in the plan's settled section, and use it unchanged in the docks section. Related: [[feedback_changelog_rules]], [[feedback_tell_when_to_commit_plans]].
