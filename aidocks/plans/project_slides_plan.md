Settled design (2026-11-11):
- A slide covers the whole belt, so you always go onto it. Its sounds are shared (misc/game, since 2026-11-11): slideground heard ahead, getting closer; slidestart as you get on, slidemove while it carries you (in place of footsteps), slidestop as you come off. No countdown for now (the dev may add one).
- On it, for a random slide_min_time to slide_max_time (2 to 4 s): your current speed halved (current_step_time(), powerups included), never past min_step_time; no footsteps and no heartbeat; jumping, crouching, hands, inventory and weapons blocked like a door carry, but lane switches work.
- Obstacles keep coming, with no countdown, and you pass through them harmlessly; passing through counts toward nothing (statistics, achievements, practice, combinations). An obstacle whose countdown began before the slide goes quiet once you're on.
- Items reaching you in whatever lane you're in are collected automatically (each counts as picked up, and as an item caught on a slide); the rest glide past. Ground powerups are run onto as usual; air powerups never appear during a slide.
- Real doors and their keys are held back until the slide ends. The landing is clear: nothing placed from the slide's end to a full countdown and the minimum floor past it.
- How often: its own roll, 1 in slide_chance (6) each spawn, and survival_roll() (easy 25, medium 20, hard 15, insane 10, chaos 5 percent; debug 100): about 1 spawn in 24 on easy to 120 on chaos. It joins at 3.3 steps a second (300 ms in schedule_types) on easy to insane, from the start on chaos; in every environment (shared sounds).
- No slides in practice runs. Debug spawn choice "slides": the full game like "all", with a slide about every other spawn, and 2 debug shields.
- Statistics: slides_ridden and slide_items_caught. Achievements: riding, lifetime 10 Slippery Start ("Slides only start once the belt reaches 3.3 steps a second, so give each run time to get going."), 50 Smooth Operator ("Slides come most often on easy and get rarer on each harder level."), 250 Slide Master ("Long runs bring more slides. Nothing can hurt you while you're on one."); one run, 3 Triple Glide ("Listen for a slide's sound ahead of you. It covers the whole belt, so you can't miss it."), 5 Slick Run ("The longer you survive, the more slides you'll ride. Easy brings the most."), 10 Ice Skater ("Ten slides in one run takes a long run. Use each one to rest your hands."); items caught, lifetime 10 Drive-By Collector ("You can switch lanes on a slide. Steer into an item's lane to catch it as you pass."), 100 Sweeper ("Listen for each item's sound in its lane as it rushes towards you, then move into it."), 500 Treasure Glider ("Every slide is a chance to grab something. Catch what you can before it ends."); one run, 5 Scoop ("Items you catch on a slide go straight into your inventory, no hands needed."), 10 Grab and Glide ("At double speed, items come fast. Move early towards the one you want."), 20 Speed Shopper ("Twenty in one run means catching nearly everything on every slide. Keep moving."). Descriptions "Ride %threshold% slides ..." and "Catch %threshold% items on slides ...", over all your runs or in one run. 104 achievements in all.
