# Helicopters plan

The todo item "Add helicopters that you must jump to shoot." Discussion opened 2026-11-19 at 2.6, 8 of 10.

## Findings

- Sounds (9926e55): <env>/transports/aircrafts: countdown warn1-1 to warn2-5 (two layers, like vehicles), general/helicopters/helicopter: loop, hurt1 to hurt4, death. No shoot, hit or impact sound yet (vehicles have shoot, hit and impact).
- The transports prep (d03ff83): environments.nvgt's transport_types holds vehicles; aircrafts joins it when helicopters are built.
- Relatives: insects (insect.nvgt) are only hit while you're crouched, and hover at your head for insect_dive_time before stinging; vehicles fire bursts down their lane (vehicle_burst_shots 5, vehicle_shot_damage 5, vehicle_burst_pause 2000) and ram.

## Settled design

- A transport (environments.nvgt's transport_types gains "aircrafts"), its own file game/helicopter.nvgt in the entity pattern (pool in all_pools, null-slot removal, destroy_all, clear_run_objects(), step_check()). Sounds: <env>/transports/aircrafts: countdown (two layers), general/helicopters/<theme>: loop, shoot, hurt1 to hurt4, death, land, hit. Each spawn a random kind, then a random theme, like vehicles.
- Flying in: timed to a meeting tile like a projectile, in a random lane, its countdown on the last steps; on its meeting step it starts hovering instead of passing, its loop moving with you.
- Hovering (helicopter_hover_time 4000): over one lane, drifting a lane toward yours every 1000 ms; a shot every 1000 ms: shoot.ogg at it, then 500 ms later the shot lands in the lane it was fired at: in that lane, 10 damage (take_damage(), level scaled, a shield first) and your hit sound; another lane, a miss.
- Hitting it: any weapon, only while you're in the air from your own jump (melee within reach, bullets fired mid-jump); hurt1 to hurt4 on each hit, death at 0 hp; hp 50 to 150 by speed (wall_spawn_hp() style); never from the ground, never during the landing.
- Landing (2000 ms): land.ogg, no more shots, not hittable, drifting a lane toward yours every 750 ms; at touchdown your lane is the crash (hit.ogg, 40 damage), another lane a clean dodge, then it's left behind.
- Carried (a slide or trampoline flight): its shots and crash can't touch you, and you can't hit it. Not in combinations.
- Schedule: last in schedule_types, joining at 200 ms. Practice: after vehicles, trampolines moved before combinations (... vehicles, helicopters, trampolines, combinations, mixed obstacles); a pass is destroyed or dodged, "get past"; hints and a death message.
- Statistics: helicopters_destroyed, helicopters_dodged, caught_helicopters, helicopter_shots_taken (session and lifetime; destroyed and dodged in timed_pass_stats). 13 achievements mirroring vehicles' (names in the Q&A).
- Debug spawn choice "helicopters"; free run's add obstacles with vehicles' lane choice; learn sounds through transport_types; a game document helicopters.txt; the template script rerun.
