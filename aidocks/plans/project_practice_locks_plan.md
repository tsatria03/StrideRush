Settled design:
- The chain follows the practice menu: hazards (always open), spikes, mines, projectiles, force fields, animals, walls, fake doors, real doors, mixed doors, barriers. Each opens after 5 clean passes of the one before it (practice_unlock_count 5 in tuning.nvgt, like difficulty_unlock_runs). Barriers have no goal.
- Goals: jump over hazards, spikes, mines; duck under projectiles; switch off force fields; scare off animals (birds or dogs); break walls; break fake doors; open real doors; get through mixed doors (a real door opened or a fake door broken).
- Counted only in practice runs, only while practicing that obstacle, only on clean passes; passes add up across runs and deaths. Stats, achievements and difficulty runs still ignore practice.
- Saved in saves/game.srf; clearing game progresses keeps them, like the difficulty unlocks.
- Menu lines: "Hazards. Available.", "Spikes. Unlocked after you jump over 5 hazards.", with "more" once the obstacle before is open and started ("2 more hazards"), singular for 1 ("1 more hazard"). Choosing a locked one: "This obstacle is unlocked after you jump over 2 more hazards." through dlgmessage_return(), the menu staying on it.
- At the fifth pass, mid-run: misc/game/pracunlock.ogg and "Obstacle unlocked: Spikes are now available for practice." in the misc buffer; the run goes on.
- The practice run's mode description ends "...and nothing you do here counts toward your progress, apart from unlocking the next obstacle to practice."
