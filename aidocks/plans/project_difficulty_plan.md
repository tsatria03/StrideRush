Today's real game (tuning.nvgt, spawner.nvgt): every run starts at 500 ms and speeds up 10 ms every 10 s to 50 ms; every obstacle type in obstacle_types (hazards, spikes, mines, force fields, projectiles, walls, fake doors, animals, barriers) is an equal pick from the first spawn, plus real doors at each 50 ms mark; 3 s spawn-ahead, 1 to 4 s of floor between obstacles; 1 life and 200 health; damages from animals 10 to barriers 80; items by their chances.

Reference (read only): ToyMania's difficulty ([[reference_toymania_scoring_difficulty]]): int gamemod 1 to 5, Easy, Medium, Hard, Insane, Chaos, picked every run by choose_difficulty() and not saved; a tier unlocks after completing the one below; effects are pure scaling (health base plus a bonus per tier, enemy health times the tier, timings divided by it). Its bugs: the difficulty name ternary repeated in about 6 places. The memory notes that for a runner it maps naturally to starting speed, speed-up rate and hazard density.

SETTLED: all three levers; speed means the starting step time only (the speed-up stays 10 ms every 10 s for everyone). Five levels, ToyMania's names: easy, medium, hard, insane, chaos.

Chaos can still be the hardest through the mix (all types from the start) and spacing. (Awaiting the dev's choice.)
- A (dev, 2026-11-05): "I'm leaning more into my second example." SETTLED: starting speeds easy 500, medium 450, hard 400, insane 350, chaos 300.

So the starting speed decides the opening mix on its own (easy starts with 3 types, medium 4, hard 5, insane 6), and everything is in play from 200 ms on any level; chaos alone ignores the schedule and has all nine from the first spawn. Each new type is a fresh equal pick in the mix when it joins. (Asked 2026-11-05; Claude recommended this.)
- A (dev, 2026-11-05): "Yes, but what about the real door markers?" SETTLED: the schedule above.

A bonus: fake doors only join at 250, so on easy, medium, hard and insane every door before then is real, letting players learn the real door's sound before they have to tell fakes apart. Chaos keeps fake doors from the first spawn, with everything else. (Asked 2026-11-05; Claude recommended this.)
- A (dev, 2026-11-05): "Yes. What about items, and which difficulties will each set appear? This includes powerups." SETTLED: real doors unchanged.
