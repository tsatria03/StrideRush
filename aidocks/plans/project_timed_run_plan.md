Settled design (2026-11-13):
- The mode menu: timed run after endless run, "Timed run. This mode gives you a countdown clock: clean passes add time, hits take it away, and you win by reaching the finish line before your time runs out." It unlocks after 5 completed easy endless runs (difficulty_runs[0] >= difficulty_unlock_runs, when medium opens); locked, "Timed run. Unlocked after completing 5 easy endless runs." counting down; choosing it locked says so in a dialog.
- Levels: easy to chaos with their own counts, separate from endless: each level opens after 3 completed timed runs on the one below; a win, a time-out or a death counts, quitting or restarting doesn't. The difficulty menu's lines and "Choose a difficulty level. N of 5 unlocked." read the timed counts in a timed run.
- Play: as an endless run on that level (speed, schedule, spacing, combinations, items, real doors and keys, damage, 200 health and 1 life), plus two clocks on runtimer (pausing freezes them): the countdown starts at easy 60 s, medium 50, hard 40, insane 30, chaos 20; the finish line is total running time, easy 5:00, medium 4:30, hard 4:00, insane 3:30, chaos 3:00. Each clean pass (any obstacle got past without a hit, a combination's parts each) adds and each hit takes away, pass/hit: easy 7/5, medium 6/5, hard 5/7, insane 4/8, chaos 4/10, all in tuning.
- Keeping track: T says "You have 45 seconds left, and 2 minutes 10 seconds to the finish line."; warnings "30 seconds left.", "10 seconds left.", "5 seconds left." in status, once each as the countdown drops past, again after it climbs back; nothing spoken per pass or hit.
- Endings: the finish line reached with time left, "You crossed the finish line."; the countdown at zero, "Time's up."; a death as in endless (a hazard fall too). Then the results: the outcome, the time left (a win) or survived, and the best finish on that level; then replay run or return to game menu, escape "canceled". Speech only; optional sounds misc/game/timewin, timelost, timewarn played if ever added.
- Records, apart from the endless statistics (stats_counting stays endless-only): per level, runs played, wins, time-outs, deaths, best finish (most time left at the finish line); a timed run records section in the statistics menu; saved in game.srf; cleared with game progress.
- Achievements (5, 135 in all), each "Reach the finish line of a timed run on <level>.": Beat the Clock (easy), Against the Clock (medium), Race Against Time (hard), Photo Finish (insane), Time Lord (chaos), hints as in the Q&A log. Timed runs earn no endless achievements.
