- Q2 (Claude, 2026-11-16): what counts as having obstacles? spawner.nvgt's obstacle_types are hazards, spikes, mines, force fields, projectiles, walls, fake doors, animals, barriers, winds, ladders, floor breakers and vehicles, and each joins a run on the difficulty schedule (has_joined()). Proposed: an environment qualifies when it has at least one of those that the run could bring at that moment, checked at each start and each switch, the same test available_obstacles() already makes. So an environment with only vehicles stays out of an easy run until vehicles join, then becomes a place to go. Real doors, slides and trampolines don't count on their own, since they come alongside obstacles rather than instead of them. Recommended: as proposed.
