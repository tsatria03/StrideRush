## Findings (2026-11-18)
- The todo item: "Add a way to choose which combination comes in a free run, from any of the pairs in either order, instead of a random one."
- tuning.nvgt's 9 pairs (combo_firsts then combo_seconds), each also reversed, so 18: projectile and spike, force field and fake wall, force field and animal, mine and barrier, barrier and projectile, fake wall and barrier, animal and hazard, fake door and mine, projectile and insect.
- combo.nvgt's choose_combo() picks at random among the pairs whose two kinds have joined and the environment has (combo_part_ready()), into next_combo_first and next_combo_second; a free run reaches it through choose_free_run() when free_next_type() gives "combinations".
- Combinations are already a free run obstacle kind (freebelt.nvgt's free_obstacle_ids), offered when the environment can make some pair. The add flow (free_add()): kind, how many, health (breakable kinds), continuous; the settled lanes plan adds a lane question before the continuous one. Combinations aren't a breakable or lane kind, so today they get kind, how many, continuous.
