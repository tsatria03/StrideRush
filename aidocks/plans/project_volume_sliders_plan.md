Background:
- The dev (2026-11-08): "In the sound tab, there should be a master volume slider, a game volume slider, and a music volume slider. Look at how the audio settings work in toy mania", then: "Oh I forgot I also want menu music volume."
- ToyMania (read only, [[reference_toymania_readonly]]), soundsetsmenu() in menus/menu.nvgt: an "audio settings" form of sliders from 0 to 100 "percent" in steps of 1, each mapped to decibels as (slider / 2) - 50, so 0 to 100 percent is -50 to 0 dB: "game alarm volume: adjustable", "game ambience volume: adjustable", "game heartbeat volume: adjustable", "game music volume: adjustable", "menu music volume: adjustable", "master game volume: adjustable". Every loop it reads all the sliders and applies them live (set_sound_master_volume(), update_sound_start_values() on the playing slots), so a change is heard at once; outside a game, test buttons play the alarm, ambience, heartbeat and music (a track list) while you adjust; master_volume is saved and set again at startup by set_sound_master_volume().
- Stride Rush now: menumusvolume (form_menu, -100 to 0 dB in steps of 2 on Page Up and Down in any menu, saved as menumusvolume); music_volume and ambience_volume (run.nvgt, -50 to 0 dB in steps of volume_change 1 on F3/F4 and F5/F6 and the volume keys mid-run, saved); no master volume, though the pinned engine has the sound_master_volume property; no game volume: every game pool (all_pools) plays at its sounds' own volumes, each with its own mixer (decpool.nvgt). The Sound tab (main menu only, hidden from F2) holds "Enable global HRTF support", "Enable global volume fading", "Enable looping of previewed sounds" and the sound device button. deps/form.nvgt has create_slider(), get_slider() and set_slider().
