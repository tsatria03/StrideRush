## Engine findings (2026-11-16)
- C:\nvgt's legacy engine exposes a joystick class built on SDL3's gamepad API (Legacy-NVGT src/input.cpp); nvgt.exe has it compiled in. The manual pages calling it a ghost or no-op object are stale.
- It works: button_pressed/down/released/up (presses from SDL events, cleared by the first check, like key_pressed), buttons_pressed() and the other list versions, sticks x/y and r_x/r_y (0 to 65535, centered 32768), triggers z and r_z, the d-pad as buttons 11 to 14 and as pov_1, vibrate, vibrate_triggers, set_led, name, power_level, list_joysticks, set(index); global joystick_count().
- SDL button numbers: 0 bottom face (Xbox A), 1 right, 2 left, 3 top, 4 back, 5 guide, 6 start, 7 left stick, 8 right stick, 9 left shoulder, 10 right shoulder, 11 to 14 d-pad up, down, left, right.
- Weak spots: only pads SDL has a mapping for are seen; only the first pad is opened, at construction, and refresh_joystick_list() does nothing (a pad plugged in later needs set(0)); no button repeat (menus need their own timed repeat); sticks and triggers are numbers, not buttons (dead zone code needed); buttons is always 26, can_vibrate and has_led are always true once connected; sliders, povs 2 to 4 and the v_/a_/f_ axes are dummies.
- The engine's input_bind class also loads joystick ini files, but the game doesn't need it.
- The game's key_config.nvgt is already joystick-ready: set_joystick(), a [joystick] section of "action=button:trigger" lines (such as "jump=0:press"), load_joystick()/save_joystick(), and action_triggered() checks the pad alongside the keyboard. load_keyboard() skips [joystick]. So run actions read through controls answer to the pad once one is set. Not ready: form_menu.nvgt reads the keyboard directly, and the sticks have no code.
