The reference (read only): the dev's CaveDefender, user/CaveDefender-main (gitignored), client menus/menu.nvgt learnsoundsmenu() and deps/form_menu.nvgt learn_game_sounds_menu() ([[reference_cavedefender_buffer]] for the folder).
- learnsoundsmenu() builds one dictionary per category (Ammo, Interface, Surface, Wall, Weapon, Wood), each mapping a spoken label to a sound path, or to an array of paths for a sound with numbered variants. Two helpers: ls_add(dict, label, path) for one file, ls_addv(dict, label, dir, base, count) for variants, which adds ". This sound has multiple variants." to the label. Every label says where the sound is used ("Pistol reload: used in EVP and free play", "Concrete step: used when you walk on concrete").
- The category menu (sorted names, then back; intro "Learn sounds menu. Choose a category of sounds, then pick a sound to preview it.") loops; escape or back says "canceled" and returns. Picking a category opens a fresh menu (intro "<category> sounds.") handed to learn_game_sounds_menu() with audio_prefix "sounds/".
- learn_game_sounds_menu(dict, prefix, menu): lists the labels sorted, then "Go back"; Enter plays the sound (a random variant for arrays), moving to another item stops it, "Go back" or escape returns to the categories. It's a stock form_menu helper.
- In CaveDefender it also mutes the game pools while open in a session; Stride Rush's game menu is never open during a run, so that part isn't needed.
- Stride Rush ALREADY HAS learn_game_sounds_menu() in src/includes/main/deps/form_menu.nvgt (same helper), unused so far.

Stride Rush's sounds (sdr/sounds, scanned 2026-11-05): objects/traps/<type>/<theme>/ for the nine obstacle types (loops, hits, spawns and so on); misc/trap/<type>/ countdown sets (warn1-5, or layered warnN-k); objects/platforms/ 127 surfaces (step, land, fall); equipments/items/common (ammo_pack, health_potion, life_capsule, player_teleporter, steel_shield) and items/other (seven key colors); equipments/weapons/melee (bat, boot, club, feat, fist, hammer, knife, laser) and artillery (machine_gun, pistol); misc/player (bump, clap, death, duck, give, heal, hit, inv, jump, land, life, rise, turn, unduck, woosh); misc/game (pause, resume, powerflight, powerget, powerground, switchpress); objects/audio (ambiences, tracks); ui (buffer, dialog, menu, other).
