Status: SETTLED 2026-11-03 (not built; sections below), started 2026-11-03 on top of 5b10a87, after the life potion and teleporter ([[project_potion_teleporter_plan]]). No code until the plan is settled and the dev gives a go-ahead ([[feedback_plans_record_qna]]). 1.2 holds 8 of 10 changelog entries ([[feedback_changelog_rules]]).

As built now ([[project_shields_plan]], lives.nvgt): each obstacle costs a fixed number of lives (spikes, force fields, projectiles and walls 1, mines 2, doors 3), checked with is_fatal() and paid with lose_lives(); a worn shield takes shield damage instead (20, mines 40, doors 60) out of its 200 hp; hazards end the run; a run starts with 1 life; a life potion adds 1.

The dev's idea (2026-11-03): "You know how shields have health? I was thinking that your player will have health as well. 200hp. The damage sent by shields will also apply to your player health as well. If your health is 0, a life gets taken away if you have more than 1. If not, you die."
- So the player has 200 hp, and an unshielded blow takes the same damage a shield would (20, mines 40, doors 60) from your hp instead of costing lives directly.
- At 0 hp you lose a life if you have more than 1; on your last life, 0 hp ends the run.

- Q: When a blow does more damage than the hp you have left (60 from a door with 20 hp left), does the extra carry over into your next life? - A: agreed (dev, 2026-11-03: "It should not carry into the next life as subtracted from your health."). Extra damage past 0 is dropped; the next life starts at 200. SETTLED.
- Q: How do you hear your health? (Asked 2026-11-03; Claude recommended H speaking both, as a percent like the shield readout: "You have 2 lives, and your health is at 70 percent.")

CORRECTED (dev, 2026-11-03: "it will say x percent health remaining, x lives. I think that's one of the formats SF used."): it is SimpleFighter's third format.
