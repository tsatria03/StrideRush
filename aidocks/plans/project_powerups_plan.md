I want to plan the last 2 interactive items, life potions and player teleporters."), so the potion and teleporter come first. Started 2026-11-03 on top of 01d5ed0, after shields, as the next 1.2 feature.

Sounds (added by the dev 2026-11-03, rescanned): misc/game/powerget, powerloop1 and powerloop2, one shared set for every powerup (no per-type folders). Presumably a powerup on the belt plays a powerloop at its tile and powerget when you run over it; the two loops are just random variety for every powerup (dev, 2026-11-03: "the loop sounds are randomized 1 and 2. It's for all powerups"), so a loop never hints whether the event is good or bad.

The dev's definition (2026-11-03), correcting Claude, who had called the life potion and player teleporter powerups:
- "Powerups are completely different from normal items that you pick up. Powerups are basicly passive items that you get by running over them, and they can grant you positive/negative events."
- So a powerup is collected by running onto it (no hand press, and it doesn't break), never goes into the inventory, and triggers an effect at once, which can be good or bad.
- "life potions and player teleporters are direct items that you can pick up. I'll explain what those will do in a later message." Those are ordinary hand-pickup inventory items like ammo packs, keys and shields (sounds in equipments/items/common), and get their own plans once the dev describes them.

Build sections (written 2026-11-03; build one per go-ahead, [[feedback_describe_dont_show_code]]):
Section 1: powerups on the belt and the effect timers.
- New globals/powerup.nvgt (poweruppool, the item entity pattern): spawn_powerup() picks ground or flight and loops powerground or powerflight at its tile; powerup_check() each step collects one you arrive on the right way (powerget centered, then the event's name spoken), and a missed one fades behind you. Weapons and bullets ignore powerups.
- On contact, pick_powerup_event() picks at random among the 13, leaving out Shield Boost and Shield Crack with no shield worn. One-offs act at once (the two shield ones). Timed ones go in one slot per kind (speed, jumps, crouches, melee, guns, footsteps), replacing what's there and restarting powerup_time (20 s) on runtimer, so pause freezes them; expiry says "<name> wore off.". reset_powerups() in reset_run().
- tuning.nvgt: powerup_chance 10, powerup_time 20000, and the effect amounts. spawner.nvgt: "powerups" in item_types with its roll; testing choice. Wiring: all_pools, clear_run_objects(), step_check().
Section 2: the effects themselves. Speed: an offset (plus or minus 50 ms) on the step interval, with the normal speed-up and the door marks underneath, never past 50 or 500. Jump and crouch lengths scaled (1.5x and 2/3x; 2x and 1/2x). Melee and gun fire gaps halved or doubled. Muffled Steps skips the footstep only.
Section 3: docks in 1.2 and the todo list.
