Sounds (added by the dev 2026-11-03, rescanned): misc/game/powerget, powerloop1 and powerloop2, one shared set for every powerup (no per-type folders). Presumably a powerup on the belt plays a powerloop at its tile and powerget when you run over it; the two loops are just random variety for every powerup (dev, 2026-11-03: "the loop sounds are randomized 1 and 2. It's for all powerups"), so a loop never hints whether the event is good or bad.

The dev's definition (2026-11-03), correcting Claude, who had called the life potion and player teleporter powerups:
- "Powerups are completely different from normal items that you pick up. Powerups are basicly passive items that you get by running over them, and they can grant you positive/negative events."
- So a powerup is collected by running onto it (no hand press, and it doesn't break), never goes into the inventory, and triggers an effect at once, which can be good or bad.
- "life potions and player teleporters are direct items that you can pick up. I'll explain what those will do in a later message." Those are ordinary hand-pickup inventory items like ammo packs, keys and shields (sounds in equipments/items/common), and get their own plans once the dev describes them.
