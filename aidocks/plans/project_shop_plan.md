# Shop plan

The todo item "Add a shop that you can access in the game to buy items and special helpers." Discussion opened 2026-11-20 at 2.7, 1 of 10. The dev: talk about what it offers, how you get there, and the currency, looking at ToyMania's store.

## Findings

- ToyMania (read only, src/includes/main/menus/menu.nvgt's shopmenu(), globals/game.nvgt):
  - Currency: money in dollars, earned only in endless mode, level times 20 for each level survived (a level every minute), with misc/player/money.ogg and "Congratulations! You earned $40 for surviving to level 2."; saved in savedata between runs; an info key speaks "You have 40 dollars available to spend."
  - Getting there: an in-game key (info_store_or_shop) during an endless run pauses the game and boss timers and opens the shop menu; back or escape says canceled and resumes.
  - The menu: "Shop menu. What would you like to buy?", one line per item found in the endless item sound folders, sorted: "ammo_pack, costs 5 dollars. You currently have 2 of these in your inventory.", then back. Prices in a dictionary (ammo pack 5, health potion 10, player teleporter 15, stun dart 20, anything else 1).
  - Buying: an input box "How many ammo_packs would you like to buy?" (digits only); errors play misc/store/error and a dialog (no money, invalid quantity, not enough money for that many) and return to the shop; success plays misc/store/buy and "Purchase complete. You bought 2 ammo packs for 10 dollars. You now have 30 dollars remaining.", then the shop again.
  - Not to copy: the item names spoken with underscores, "Canceled" capitalised, the shop reopening by recursion, money as a double.
- Stride Rush's store sounds already exist (sdr/sounds/main/misc/store): open, close, purchase, buy1 to buy4, error.
- Stride Rush's inventory items: ammo packs, health potions, keys (by color), life capsules, player teleporters, steel shields; specials: powerups (several kinds) and slides. The free run console can already give each of them (freerun.nvgt), which a shop could reuse.

## Settled design

- Coins (never dollars): earned only in endless runs, one payment per clean pass (timed_pass_stats' keys), easy 20, medium 40, hard 60, insane 80, chaos 100 (tuning.nvgt), silent; the balance saved in saves/game.srf between runs; Y (info_coins) says "You have 1,250 coins."; clear game progresses clears them (its confirmation says so), clearing statistics doesn't.
- Statistics: coins_earned and coins_spent, session and lifetime, in the statistics menu and session results.
- Getting there: shop=BACKSLASH,F8 ([screens]; joystick unbound), only in an endless run; it pauses the run, open.ogg through dlgplay(), and close.ogg through dlgplay() on leaving, the run resuming.
- The shop (CookieCraze's flow): items, specials, back, its intro saying your coins; a line "health potion, costs 1,000 coins. You can buy 3. You have 2."; a pack menu: buy 1, 5, 10, 25, 50, 100, then "Buy all <items> for <price>", then back; a purchase is spoken and keeps you on its line, buy all or a purchase leaving you unable to afford another shows a dialog and returns to the list; a pack you can't afford plays error.ogg with a one-sentence dialog; the pack menu won't open if you can't afford one. The helpers have no pack menu: choosing one buys it, and owning one already says "You already have the item helper." with error.ogg.
- Purchase sounds: a random buy1 to buy4, purchase.ogg and a coin sound together, the coin by the total spent: under 1,000 coin1, 1,000 to 2,499 coin50, 2,500 to 4,999 coin1000, 5,000 to 9,999 coin10000, 10,000 to 19,999 coin50000, 20,000 and up coin100000.
- Items (no keys): ammo pack 500, health potion 1,000, steel shield 1,500, player teleporter 1,500, life capsule 5,000. Specials, new inventory items: slide 1,000, trampoline 1,500, item helper 4,000, door helper 4,000. Prices in tuning.nvgt.
- Specials in use (the use key): the slide starts a slide at once; the trampoline launches a good trampoline's flight from where you stand (the environment's good trampoline sounds); neither while a slide or flight carries you ("You're already sliding.", "You're already in the air.", kept). The helpers toggle ("Item helper on."/"off.", ui/other toggleon/toggleoff; inventory line "item helper, on"), last the whole run, through lost lives, gone when the run ends: the item helper takes each item in your lane, keys too, as its hand window opens; the door helper uses your first key on a locked real door as it comes within key reach and opens it as its hand window opens, ignoring fake doors, nothing without a key.
- Free runs: modify player inventory's give, take and set items list the four specials; add items leaves them out; no shop.
- Achievements (9, 172 to 181): coins earned over all runs 10,000 Pocket Change, 100,000 Piggy Bank, 1,000,000 Treasure Chest; in one run 2,500 Payday, 5,000 Jackpot, 10,000 Gold Rush; coins spent over all runs 10,000 Regular Customer, 100,000 Big Spender, 1,000,000 Shopaholic.
- Changelog, on top in this order: "Added coin statistics and nine new achievements for earning and spending coins, and the shop's specials can now be given from a free run's console." / "Added specials to the shop, a slide and a trampoline you can use whenever you like, and an item helper and a door helper you switch on to grab items in your lane and unlock and open real doors for you." / "Added a shop, opened with backslash or F8 during an endless run, where coins you earn by getting past obstacles buy items such as health potions and life capsules, while Y tells you how many coins you have."
- Sounds: all present (main/misc/store: open, close, purchase, buy1 to buy4, error, the six coins; ui/other toggleon/toggleoff; slides' and trampolines' own).
