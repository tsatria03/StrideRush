## Findings (2026-11-16)
- Engine (Legacy-NVGT src/input.cpp): joystick.vibrate(uint16 low_frequency, uint16 high_frequency, int duration) is SDL_RumbleGamepad: two motors, each 0 to 65535, for duration ms; a new call replaces the one running. vibrate_triggers(left, right, duration) is the Xbox trigger motors (SDL_RumbleGamepadTriggers). can_vibrate and can_vibrate_triggers are always true once a pad is open, so a pad with no motor just ignores it, and the game can't hide the setting for it.
- The game: globals/joystick.nvgt holds joy (null without joystick.ini). Every blow but a hazard or floor breaker's fall goes through take_damage() in lives.nvgt, which returns blow_shielded (a worn shield took it), blow_hurt, blow_life_lost or blow_fatal. Run-ending deaths: end_run_hazard() and end_run_floor() (falls) and end_run_hit() (a fatal blow), all in endrun.nvgt; practice deaths go through practice_death().
- DualSense (PS5), researched at the dev's request (2026-11-16): it has no rumble motors but voice-coil haptics, which SDL drives as emulated rumble through the same SDL_RumbleGamepad, so joy.vibrate() works on it; its adaptive triggers and finer haptics aren't reachable through the engine. Over USB rumble works; over Bluetooth, SDL before 3.2 (the engine's headers say 3.1.2) needs its PS5 rumble hint for rumble, which defaults off, so Bluetooth rumble may not work: to be checked if the dev tests one. SDL's buttons for it: cross 0 (the game's A button), circle 1 (B), square 2 (X), triangle 3 (Y), create 4 (back), PS 5 (guide), options 6 (start), L3 and R3 7 and 8, L1 and R1 9 and 10 (the shoulders), the d-pad 11 to 14, the mic mute 15, the touchpad click 20; L2 and R2 are triggers, axes the game doesn't use.
