Status: SETTLED (2026-11-12), waiting for the dev to commit it before Section 1. Started 2026-11-12 with 2.0 at 13 of 20 and no other plan open.

Background:
- The dev's four practice suggestions (2026-11-12, "Please put them all in a plan."):
  1. Weapons should not be available for obstacles that don't need them. Pressing the number row and space should do nothing there, and no weapon should be drawn.
  2. If you die on a practice run, the dialog should say more about how to get past the obstacle, instead of simply "You died."
  3. During a practice run, when an obstacle or key appears, it should say so and what to do to get it or pass it, something like "A key has appeared. Turn left or right, and use a hand to grab it." Only 5 times, just like with any obstacle.
  4. If you die, a menu should ask whether you want to try again, but only after the death dialog has been dismissed.
- Reference for item 3: the dev's Windows port of a cave lane game, in user/InsideTheCave-Windows-main (gitignored, read only; the original game isn't the dev's, so it is never named in committed files). Its tutorial (insidethecave/game/game_scene.py, ARRIVAL, announcement(), announce(), _caught(), watchPassing()): each kind of thing is announced as it appears, naming its lane and what to do ("A torch appeared in the left lane. Go there to pick it up."; "A monster appeared in the middle lane. Stay out of it, or throw your torch at it."), an extra clause only while that action is possible, each line interrupting; a pickup is confirmed ("Coin caught."), and a thing that passes you untaken is said. Each kind in each lane is announced once, 12 lines in all, then a closing line and silence ("It gives people the feel for what's going to be in a level without bombarding them with so much info over and over."). It speaks through SAPI there; Stride Rush speaks through the screen reader (speak_buffer()).
- Today's practice (the game menu refactor, practice deaths and practice locks plans): practice_death() in endrun.nvgt pauses, plays the fall or the death, says "You died." in dlgmessage_return(), waits for Enter's release, then sets you down past the obstacle with full health, 1 life and the practice shield; weapons work as in an endless run, with unlimited reserve; practice_pass() counts clean passes toward the next lock (practice_unlock_count 5).

Settled design (2026-11-12):
- No practice line ever names a keyboard key, only actions (keys can be rebound).
- Weapons (item 1): kept in practice runs of walls, fake doors, mixed doors, combinations and trampolines. In the other nine (hazards, spikes, mines, projectiles, force fields, animals, real doors, barriers, winds) no weapon is drawn, and the number row, space, R and X do nothing, silently.
- The practice buffer: a new speech buffer, practice, in buffer_names (alphabetical: achievements, doors, items, misc, powerups, practice, status, weapons), for the hints and the closing line. The "Obstacle unlocked" and "Mode unlocked" lines stay in misc.
- Hints (item 3): as each thing appears in a practice run, the practice buffer says what appeared, then what to do, two sentences, interrupting (but waiting behind an unlock line just said). Each kind gets its first 5 appearances in each practice run, starting over every run. A thing in one lane names its exact lane (left, middle or right), never "your lane"; belt-wide things name none. The lines: hazard "A hazard has appeared. Jump just before you reach the edge."; spike and mine "... Jump as its last countdown cue plays."; projectile "... Crouch before it reaches you, and stay down until it passes."; force field "... Press either hand during its countdown to switch it off."; animal, a bird or a dog "... Clap with both hands to scare it away."; wall "... Break it with your weapons before you reach it."; fake door "... It won't open, so break it with your weapons."; real door "A real door has appeared. Use a key from your inventory to unlock it, then open it with a hand."; key "A key has appeared in the left lane. Move into its lane and use a hand to grab it."; barrier "A barrier has appeared in the middle lane. Stay out of its lane, or switch out before it reaches you."; wind "A wind has appeared in the right lane. Wait for its last move, then switch lanes so it can't follow."; good lane trampoline "A good trampoline has appeared in the middle lane. Move into its lane to fly over what comes next."; worn lane trampoline "A worn trampoline has appeared in the left lane. Stay out of its lane, since it only gives a short bounce."; good belt-wide trampoline "A good belt-wide trampoline has appeared. You can't avoid it, so get ready to fly."; worn belt-wide trampoline "A worn belt-wide trampoline has appeared. You can't avoid it, so get ready for a short bounce."
- Counting: each kind its own 5 per run. The closing line comes after the practiced choice's fifth hint, once that thing has passed you: real doors after the fifth door (keys 5 of their own), mixed doors after the fifth door of either kind, combinations after the fifth pair (each part hinted by its kind), trampolines after the fifth trampoline of any kind (each kind and each obstacle between 5 of their own). The closing line, in the practice buffer, for every choice: "Well done, that's all the hints for this run, so keep practicing at your own pace and trust your ears."
- Death dialog (item 2): "You died.", then what caught you, then how to get past it, one line per obstacle (a fall: "You died. You fell into a hazard. Jump just before you reach the edge."); in combinations the part that caught you, in trampolines the obstacle between.
- After the dialog (item 4): a menu, "What would you like to do now?", with retry practice (set down just past what caught you, full health, the life and the shield, as now) and return to game menu (ends the run, as quit run); Escape says "canceled" and returns to the game menu, as the after death menu does.
- Docks: one 2.0 changelog entry per suggestion (four, 13 to 17 of 20); the readme's practice section.
