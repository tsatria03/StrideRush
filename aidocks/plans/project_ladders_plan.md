Settled design (2026-11-12):
- Walls become two kinds, like doors. Fake walls are today's glass walls, broken by weapons, now played at an odd pitch (fake_door_pitches: 80, 90, 110 or 120). Real walls are tall, at pitch 100, can't be broken (weapons do nothing to them), and are climbed by the ladder placed just before each one; a real wall never comes without its ladder. Player-facing, "walls" becomes "fake walls" everywhere (practice menu, statistics labels, readme, learn sounds, achievement descriptions and hints, the debug choice); saved statistic keys don't change.
- Sounds: the ladder from environments/<env>/traps/ladders/general/<theme> (metal, in default and custom): spawn as it's placed, loop as you near it, grab1-2 and climb1-5 as you climb, break, fall, land. The real wall's own loop at pitch 100 from its environment's walls themes, just past the ladder; its hit on a crash. Ladders count down (reversed 2026-11-12, see the log) with the layered traps/ladders/countdown set, until grabbed.
- Getting on: a hand press in a key's window (hand_window_start()) grabs the ladder, the first rung. Arriving without having grabbed runs you into it: break, 40, then the real wall's crash, 50.
- Climbing: the belt holds you (no steps, no step timer) while you climb the rest of its rungs (a random 6 to 8 on easy up to 10 to 12 on chaos, changed after testing; first 5) hand over hand, each press the other hand from the last, a grab and a climb sound per rung, at your own pace. The same hand twice, or longer than the rung time on one rung, collapses it: break, 40, then the wall's 50. The rung time by difficulty: easy 2 s, medium 1.75, hard 1.5, insane 1.25, chaos 1; practice and debug 2. Any lane key while climbing: fall, 40, then the wall's 50. Jumping, crouching, weapons and inventory do nothing until you land. After the fifth rung, over the top: land as you come down on clear belt past the wall, running again. A failed climb leaves you past the wall too, after the crash.
- Damage is scaled by difficulty and taken by a worn shield first, like every blow; only hazards end a run outright.
- In the run: ladders (a ladder and its real wall, one obstacle) are an equal pick joining at 250 ms (4 steps a second), from the start on chaos; the spawner keeps the ladder alone (nothing still counting down as you reach it) and a clear landing past the wall, like slides. Not in combinations; fake walls stay in today's wall pairs. On a slide or a good trampoline's flight, the ladder and wall pass harmlessly (carried()), counting nothing.
- Statistics: real_walls_climbed (a clean climb), ladders_broken (lost grip or ran into it), ladder_falls, caught_real_walls.
- Achievements (12, 130 in all): Rung by Rung 10, Over the Top 50, Wall Scaler 250 (climbs over all your runs); Up and Over 3, Ladder Runner 5, Rooftop Runner 10 (climbs in one run); Loose Grip 10, Butterfingers 100, Collapse Artist 500 (ladders broken); Wrong Way Down 10, Leaning Tower 100, Ladder Diver 500 (ladder falls); descriptions and hints as in the Q&A log.

Mixed walls are fake and real at random, half and half. Real walls practice has no weapons; mixed walls keeps them. A one-time check opens real and mixed walls on saves with fake doors open.

Grab its ladder with a hand as you reach it, then climb it hand over hand."; mixed walls each kind 5, the closing after the fifth wall of either kind. Deaths: fake wall "You died.
