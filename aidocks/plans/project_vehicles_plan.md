Settled design (2026-11-13, for 2.2):
- A new obstacle type, vehicles, from environments/<env>/vehicles: kinds as folders under general (cars), each kind's themes below (mini_car, mini_car2, mini_car3: loop, fire, hurt, death); each spawn a kind (an equal share), then one of its themes; named by kind ("a car"). Countdown from vehicles/countdown (layered), played only while it's in your lane.
- Movement: appears in a random lane, closes 1.5 times as fast as a still obstacle (like a wind), and moves one lane toward yours every second until its last move, so a late switch leaves it to drive past (vehicles_dodged).
- Firing: while in your lane, a burst of 5 shots a fifth of a second apart (fire at the car, each a shot flying down its lane), then a 2 s pause; each shot that reaches you still in that lane does 5 damage (vehicle_shots_taken); in another lane they miss.
- Breaking it: weapons hit it only while it's in your lane; health half a fake wall's (wall_spawn_hp() growth, 25 to 250, scaled by level); hurt per hit, death at 0, gone with its shots (vehicles_destroyed).
- The ram: reaching you in its lane, 40 damage (caught_vehicles), scaled by level, a shield first.
- Joins at 5 steps a second (200 ms), an equal pick, chaos from the start; not in combinations; passes harmlessly, shots too, on a slide or a good trampoline's flight.
- Statistics vehicles_destroyed, vehicles_dodged, caught_vehicles ("times hit by vehicles"), vehicle_shots_taken.
- Achievements (13, as in the Q&A log): destroyed lifetime 10, 100, 500; destroyed in one run 3, 5, 10; dodged lifetime 10, 100; hit lifetime 10, 100, 500; shots taken lifetime 100, 1000.
- Practice: after winds, before combinations (dodge 5 winds opens vehicles; get past 5 vehicles, destroyed or dodged, opens combinations); weapons drawn; a one-time check opening vehicles on saves with combinations open; mixed obstacles gains vehicles.
- Practice lines as in the Q&A log; a debug spawn choice "vehicles".
