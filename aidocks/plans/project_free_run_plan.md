Background:

- CaveDefender's free play (read only, user/CaveDefender-main/aidocks/game-mode-free.md): its 4th mode, listed last, a solo sandbox with no timer, score, win or loss, escalation or persistence (never touches wins or best records), and a practice space. Two keys: Enter for the arena's state (begin arena, enable wall damage, one-way), Tab for cavern controls (add bots by count and wall, add items by type, kind and count, clear bots, clear items, clear the whole map). Every toolkit at once, though ammo stays gathered, not unlimited ("the gathering loop is the fun"). Walls self-heal, so a solo player is never left with nothing to do.
- Stride Rush today: endless (five levels, statistics, achievements, unlocks), timed (its own levels and records), practice (one obstacle, a fixed or rising speed, hints, practice deaths, an unlock chain), and the source-only debug run (environment, spawn, speed, footsteps, lives that can't end; hidden from players, never in the docks). A free run would be close kin to the debug run, made for players.

- The setup: speed (2, 2.5, 3.3, 5, 10, rising, or normal, which speeds up as an endless run), then environment (random, switching every 1 to 2 minutes, or one to stay in). Then you stand still on an empty belt: music and ambience only, no footsteps or heartbeat. Standing, lanes, drawing weapons, the inventory, the readouts and the pause menu work; jump, crouch and hands do nothing.
- The console's order (dev, 2026-11-13: "The menu items in that console should be in alphabetical order if possible, with back at the bottum"), in two groups (dev: "I like that a lot.", then the player options moved to running only): always shown: modify death mode, modify environment, modify practice hints, modify running speed, view current setup; only while running: add items, add obstacles, clear belt, clear items, clear obstacles, modify ammo reserves (with a gun drawn), modify player health, modify player lives, modify shield strength (with a shield worn). Standing: modify death mode, modify environment, modify practice hints, modify running speed, start running, view current setup, back. Running: the belt actions and the settings, alphabetical, then back. The numbers below name the items, not their order.
- The console: a new action on backspace and F7, rebindable, doing nothing outside a free run. It opens at any time, the run frozen while it's open, changes applying as it closes. Its items, lowercase:
  1. start running (only while standing): the belt moves, footsteps and heartbeat begin. clear belt (only while running, replacing stop running, dev, 2026-11-13): every queued obstacle and item, everything on its way and every continuous rule cleared, whatever you're in cancelled, set down standing, footsteps and heartbeat stop, the console back to its standing items; settings kept.
  2. add obstacles: a kind (every obstacle, combinations, slides, trampolines) or "All obstacles" (a random mix), "How many?", for fake walls, fake doors and vehicles "How much health should each one have?" (empty for the normal health for your speed), then "Do you want these obstacles to spawn continuously?": no, that batch at the usual spacing then quiet, joining the end of the queue; yes, that kind keeps coming, its count its share against other continuous kinds. Real doors each with their key just before, changing no speed.
  3. add items: a kind (health potions, steel shields, ammo packs, life capsules, player teleporters, keys, powerups) or "All items", "How many?", "Do you want these items to spawn continuously?".
  4. clear obstacles: a menu of the kinds coming (queued or continuous) plus "All obstacles": that kind's queue, what's on its way and its continuous rule gone.
  5. clear items: the same for items.
  6. modify running speed (renamed from modify speed, dev, 2026-11-13): the setup's choices.
  7. modify death mode: "Choose what happens when you die.": normal deaths (the run ends, replay run starts the setup over) or practice deaths (freeze, the death and practice's lines, retry or return; retry sets you down past what caught you, health and lives refilled to your set amounts, inventory and everything added kept); the current one marked selected; practice deaths to start.
  8. modify practice hints: on or off, off to start; on, each added kind's hint for its first 5 appearances in the practice buffer, a hit starting them over, no closing line, stop running resetting the counts. Independent of the death mode.
  9. modify environment: the setup's choices.
  10. modify player health: "How much health do you want?", your full health set and filled (200 normally).
  11. modify player lives: "How many lives do you want?", up to 99; capsules add up to the higher of 20 or it.
  12. modify shield strength: only while a shield is worn; "How strong should your shield be?", its full strength (400 normally), M reading its percent of it.
  13. modify ammo reserves: only while the machine gun or pistol is drawn, for that gun: unlimited (the start) or set a number, "How many rounds do you want in reserve?", 0 to 999.
  14. view current setup: speed, death mode, hints, environment, each kind coming (left in its batch, or continuous with its share).
  15. back.
- Numbers: counts, health, shield strength 1 to 999, lives 1 to 99, a reserve 0 to 999; anything else says "Please type a number from 1 to 999." (or its range) and asks again; escape cancels with no change.
- The run: an empty inventory, no shield, weapons drawn; no statistics, achievements, records or unlocks; the pause menu as elsewhere, restart run back to the free run setup, quit run to the game menu.
