Build sections (written 2026-11-04; build one per go-ahead, [[feedback_describe_dont_show_code]]):
Section 1: lanes and moving.
- A new lane state (0 left, 1 middle, 2 right), reset to the middle each run. lane_left and lane_right (Left/Q, Right/E, already in keyboard.ini) move one lane at once, any time but while a door carries you: turn plays from the new lane, bump plays at an edge and you stay put. L speaks "Left lane.", "Middle lane." or "Right lane." (info_lane in keyboard.ini). Teleports, throws and door rides keep your lane.
- Panning: a lane_pan(lane) helper for anything in one lane (relative to yours), so things in all lanes stay centred.
Section 2: barriers.
- New globals/barrier.nvgt (the entity pattern): placed in your lane at spawn time, looping a random theme's loop from that lane, with the layered misc/trap/barriers countdown; arriving on its tile in its lane is a blow through take_damage(barrier_damage, a tuning value to set when built) with its hit; in another lane it passes and fades. Jumping, crouching, weapons and hands don't affect it.
- "barriers" joins obstacle_types as an equal pick, so the debug spawn choice comes with it; all_pools, clear_run_objects(), step_check() and the countdown calls.
- Added after the plan was committed (dev, 2026-11-04: "What happens if you are on another lane? How will warning cues work for this trap?"). A: "exactly right. yes": in another lane a barrier passes harmlessly, its loop carrying on from its own lane and fading behind you; its layered countdown plays only on steps where you're in its lane, centred at your position like every trap's, so the cues stop once you've moved out and start again on the next step if you move back in. SETTLED, part of Section 2.
- GO-AHEAD (dev, 2026-11-04, after powerups: "Let's start on section 1."). SECTION 1 BUILT 2026-11-04 on top of 68fd48a; awaiting tests. As built in globals/lane.nvgt: lane_left 0, lane_middle 1, lane_right 2, lane_names, current_lane (reset_lane() in reset_run()); lane_pan(lane) gives (lane - 1) * lane_pan_amount; lane_input() runs from run_input() after inventory_input(): L (info_lane, new in keyboard.ini [info]) speaks "Left lane." and so on at any time, and while no door carries you Left/Q and Right/E call move_lane(), which plays turn at the new lane's pan or, past an edge, bump at the pan of the side you tried. tuning.nvgt: lane_pan_amount 50 (Claude's starting value). Nothing else uses lanes yet.
