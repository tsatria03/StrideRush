Q&A log:
- Q: What does the life potion do when used with U? (Asked 2026-11-03; Claude guessed one extra life.) A: "Exactly right. This item gives you 1 life, no more no less." (dev, 2026-11-03). U on a life potion adds exactly 1 life and uses it up. SETTLED.
- Q: What does the player teleporter do when used with U? (Asked 2026-11-03.) The dev asked what it does in ToyMania first.
- ToyMania reference (read only; globals/inventory.nvgt, the player_teleporter branch of item use): it plays the item's use sound, removes one from the inventory, moves you to a completely random spot on the map (random x from 0 to maxx, random y from 0 to maxy) and says "Moved to x, y.". There's no safety check, so it can drop you anywhere, even next to danger. It also costs 15 in the shop (menu.nvgt prices). Stride Rush has a one-way belt with no map and no y, so a random spot can't carry over directly: the obvious version is a jump forward along the belt. Q: Should U on a teleporter carry you forward along the belt, past whatever is coming? (Asked 2026-11-03.)
- A (dev, 2026-11-03): "It's supposed to skip you forward and back at random, but we need to discuss how far." So U moves you forward OR back along the belt, at random. SETTLED on direction. Q: How far? (Asked 2026-11-03; Claude recommended measuring it in time like weapon reach, so it feels the same at any speed: a random 2 to 5 seconds of belt, turned into tiles at the current speed, forward or back half and half, never back past the start of the run.)
- A: yes (dev, 2026-11-03), adding: "you need to be very careful with this item, as it has a chance of taking you close to somewhere dangerous." So, like ToyMania's, it's a risky item by design, with no safety check that keeps you away from obstacles. SETTLED: a random 2 to 5 s of belt (in tiles at the current speed), forward or back half and half, never before tile 0.
- Q: Can it land you exactly ON an obstacle's tile, or only near one? (Asked 2026-11-03; Claude recommended near only: a landing spot right on an obstacle moves one more step the same way, so you can still land a step before a spike or mine with no time to react, but never inside one, which would otherwise count as hitting it with no warning at all.)
- A (dev, 2026-11-03): "It can never land you exactly on the obstacle, like on top of a hazard." SETTLED: a landing tile holding any obstacle (hazards included) moves on a step in the same direction until it's clear.
- Q: Are Claude's proposed defaults for the rest OK (asked 2026-11-03)? 1. Spawning like the other items: life potions about 1 in 20, teleporters about 1 in 16 (life_potion_chance, teleporter_chance), plus "life potions" and "player teleporters" testing spawn choices. 2. Pickup, breaking and the spoken names ("life potion", "player teleporter") work like ammo packs. 3. Potion: use plays centered with the player's life sound, and it says "You now have 3 lives."; no limit on lives. 4. Teleporter: use plays centered, any jump or crouch ends, you land with the landing sounds of the new tile, and it says "You were moved forward 6 steps." or "back 6 steps."; U while a door is carrying you says "You can't teleport right now." and keeps it. 5. Going back puts you in front of things you've already passed: anything still waiting on the belt counts again (a spike swings, a hazard still has to be jumped), but anything already dealt with stays that way (a broken wall, an opened door, a switched-off field, a mine that went off). 6.
