Suggestion 1: how often the ground surface changes.
- Dev (2026-11-03): "When does the tile folder change again? I feel like it doesn't change very often."
- As built ([[project_run_plan]]): surface.nvgt picks a new random surface (never ledge, never the current one) after a random surface_min_tiles 50 to surface_max_tiles 200 tiles. Because it's counted in tiles, the time between changes depends on speed: 25 to 100 s at 500 ms, 10 to 40 s at 200 ms, and 2.5 to 10 s at 50 ms. Early in a run the ground can stay the same for well over a minute.
- Q: Should surfaces change more often, and should the stretch be counted in time instead of tiles? (Asked 2026-11-03; Claude recommended counting in time, a random 10 to 30 s at any speed.) A: yes, in time, "not how many tiles you have traveled" (dev, 2026-11-03). Plan: surface_min_time 10000 and surface_max_time 30000 replace surface_min_tiles and surface_max_tiles. surface.nvgt times each stretch with a timer that pauses with the game (or off runtimer), not by me_x. SETTLED, not built.

Suggestion 2: a full 5-step warning countdown at every speed.
- Dev (2026-11-03): "make the warning sounds play at 5 steps regardless of the movement speed for all traps in the traps folder." So hazards, spikes, mines, force fields and projectiles always play warn1 to warn5, one per step, instead of countdown_steps() shrinking from 5 at 500 ms to 1 at 100 ms and faster.
- This reverses the 2026-11-02 "one step per 100 ms" rule ([[project_obstacles_plan]]). countdown_steps() becomes a constant, countdown_length 5 in tuning.nvgt, replacing countdown_ms.
- Knock-on effects, checked by Claude:
  - Hazard ledges become 5 tiles at every speed (they cover the countdown). At 500 ms that's the same as now. At faster speeds the ledge is longer than before, and a jump is in the air for about 1 s. Jumping on the cue k steps before the hazard clears it only if k times step_time is under 1000 ms. So below 200 ms any of the 5 cues works. At 200 ms warn1 is borderline. At 500 ms you jump on the last two, at 400 ms the last two, at 300 ms the last three.
  - The force field switch and item pickup window keep their half-second minimum (switch_min_time), which is longer than 5 steps below 100 ms.
  - Spacing and spawning already reserve the countdown, so the gaps grow slightly at high speed.
  - Ammo packs share the countdown length for their pickup window and spacing, even though they play no cues.
  - At 50 ms the five cues come 50 ms apart, so they may run together. To listen for in testing.
  - The readme's countdown wording ("At slower speeds the ledge is longer", cues shrinking with speed) needs updating with the 1.1 docks.
