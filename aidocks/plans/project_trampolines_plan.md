Settled design (2026-11-11):
- Four kinds of pad: good or worn (worn half the time), in one lane or across the belt (belt-wide 1 in 4). Themes from environments/<env>/traps/trampolines/general/pad to pad5: spawn as it appears, one of loop1 to loop3 as you approach (a lane pad's in its lane, a belt-wide pad's straight ahead), rise as you launch, land as you come down. Worn pads play every sound at an odd pitch like fake doors (80, 90, 110 or 120; good at 100). No countdown (the folder is empty).
- Only a grounded arrival on the pad's tile (in its lane for a lane pad) launches you; jumping over a pad avoids it. On a slide you pass through it.
- A good pad: a fixed 2.5 s flight (trampoline_air_time). Anything you reach before the landing tile passes under you, silent (no countdown) and counting toward nothing; anything after the landing tile counts down as normal while you're in the air; no enforced clear landing. In the air you can switch lanes, use your hands, fire and use your inventory; jumping and crouching wait until you land; air powerups you fly through are collected. You land with land (at the pad's pitch) and the tile's landing.
- A worn pad: a short bounce, a normal 1 s jump at the worn pitch, clearing only what a jump clears.
- How often: like slides, 1 in trampoline_chance (6) each spawn and survival_roll() (about 1 in 24 on easy to 120 on chaos), joining at 3.3 steps a second (300 ms) on easy to insane, from the start on chaos; only in environments with a trampolines theme (custom has no loops yet). Not in combinations.
- Practice: "trampolines" last, after combinations; combinations get the goal combinations_cleared ("clear", "Trampolines. Unlocked after you clear 5 combinations."); every other spawn a pad of any kind, with obstacles between; a one-time check (practice_trampolines_checked) opens trampolines on saves that already have combinations open.
- Debug: "trampolines" plays like "all" with a pad about every other spawn and 2 debug shields.
- Statistics trampoline_flights and worn_bounces. Achievements: flights over all your runs ("Take %threshold% trampoline flights over all your runs."): Bounce House 10 ("A good trampoline plays at its normal pitch. Steer into its lane to be launched."), Frequent Flyer 50 ("Trampolines come most often on easy and get rarer on each harder level."), Cloud Walker 250 ("Everything you fly over passes under you. Use the time in the air to get ready for what comes after."); in one run ("Take %threshold% trampoline flights in one run."): Spring Step 3 ("Trampolines start once the belt reaches 3.3 steps a second."), Skyward 5 ("You can switch lanes and fire while you're in the air, so line up for your landing."), Trampoline Artist 10 ("Ten flights in one run takes a long run and a good ear for which pads are worth it."); worn bounces over all your runs ("Fall for %threshold% worn trampolines over all your runs."): Dud Bounce 10 ("A worn trampoline sounds higher or lower than a good one, like a fake door."), Saggy Springs 50 ("A worn bounce is only a normal jump, so it won't carry you past much."), Worn Out 250 ("Some trampolines cover the whole belt. When one sounds worn, get ready to land almost at once."). 118 in all.
